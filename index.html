About this map

What is on this map?

Every home sale recorded in King County, Washington between May 2014 and May 2015 — 21,612 sales, from the public Kaggle dataset “House Sales in King County, USA”. It does not include homes currently for sale, rentals, anything built or sold since 2015, or any street address or photo. Each sale appears only as a point.

What does “possibly undervalued” mean?

A linear regression model predicts what each home should have sold for, from its size, grade, age, location and similar features. A sale is marked possibly undervalued when the price actually paid came in below that prediction. Only sales at $500,000 or less are included, and only ones held out of the model’s training data, so the model had never seen them when it guessed — 1,473 sales in all.

How far can I trust it?

Not far on any single home. The model cannot see condition, finishes, lot problems, or who the buyer and seller were, and some of the largest gaps are simply its own mistakes: one sale here went for $472,000 against a prediction of $1.1 million, which says more about the model than about the house. The comparison was built to look across this one dataset and should not be used outside that context. Treat a large gap as a reason to look closer, not as proof of a bargain.
